GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered beautifully.

A few simple answers before you begin. If you have something more specific in mind, just message us on WhatsApp.

How do I place a gifting request?+

Tell us what you are celebrating, who the gift is for, your preferred budget or idea, and any special details. We’ll discuss the possibilities with you on WhatsApp and help shape the gift.

Start on WhatsApp →
Can I request my own design?+

Yes. Share your reference, theme, colours, message, occasion or even a rough idea. We can discuss a custom gifting concept around it.

Can I send a gift anonymously?+

Yes. Anonymous gifting is available for suitable requests. Your identity can remain private unless you choose to reveal it. Delivery details and the gifting instructions will be confirmed privately with you.

Can I schedule a gift for a future date?+

Yes. Tell us the occasion, preferred date and location in advance. We’ll confirm what can be arranged and the delivery details with you.

What occasions do you create gifts for?+

We create gifting concepts for marriages, anniversaries, birthdays, Rakhi, Diwali, Christmas, Eid, festive occasions, personal milestones, appreciation moments and more.

Do you offer corporate gifting?+

Yes. We can discuss employee gifting, client appreciation, onboarding, milestones, festive gifting, rewards and other corporate occasions. Message us with your approximate quantity and requirements.

Discuss corporate gifting →
Where can I see your gifting work?+

Our Home page Gifting Journal is our visual gallery for selected creations, gifting moments, customer feedback and other work-related photographs. We’ll keep adding new moments as the collection grows.

How do I contact The Veil Store?+

You can message us directly on WhatsApp at +91 73806 70402, email us at theveilstore.mumbai@gmail.com, or use the Contact Us page.
